Sreya Paati Together

Tradition

What works here. Dots scatter around your cursor, finger or camera motion. Loops draw themselves along your path, a second finger or a second moving person acts as Paati, and each loop plays a soft note when sound is on. The sunrise slider clears the floor.

What is simulated. Cursor, touch or camera motion stands in for footsteps. The real installation would read feet with a floor-facing depth camera and project the light with a short-throw projector. Patterns are simplified generative drawings inspired by each tradition, not traditional designs.
